Questions, answered.

Is this a real bank?

No. Tega Bank is a personal finance tracker. It records the money you add and send, it does not move real funds.

Who can see my transactions?

Only you. Row-level security means each account can access its own data and nothing else.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts from phone to tablet to desktop, with a side menu on larger screens.

Can I try it without entering data?

Yes. After signing up, load sample transactions from your home page with one tap.
